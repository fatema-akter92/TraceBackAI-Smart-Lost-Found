Intelligent Architecture

Why TraceBackAI is Different

Not just another social media group. A full-stack smart recovery pipeline with cryptographic and AI-backed security.

AI Smart Multi-Criteria Matching

Evaluates image similarity, category, color, brand, description semantics, time delta, and GPS distance to generate match probability scores.

SecretProof™ Verification

Protects rightful owners. Claimants must submit confidential proof (scratches, partial serials, invoice details) reviewed privately by desk staff.

QR-Based Custody Tracking

Institutional desks generate dynamic QR codes to track chain of custody from initial drop-off, locker storage, to verified owner handover.

JourneyTrace™ Transit Recovery

Specifically designed for bus lines, trains, and metro rail. Matches items by route, boarding and drop-off stations, and departure times.

Bangla Voice Assistant

Integrated speech-to-text allowing users to describe their lost belongings in Bengali or English, automatically auto-filling form attributes.

Privacy-Safe Anonymous Chat

Finder and seeker communicate through shielded pseudonym channels without ever disclosing personal phone numbers or home addresses.
