AI-Powered Network

TraceBack AI

"More Than Lost & Found — A Smarter Way to Recover."

Every day, thousands of valuable personal items—laptops, smartphones, wallets, student IDs, calculators, and books—are misplaced across universities, transit hubs, and public spaces. TraceBack AI replaces scattered social media posts with a unified, intelligent recovery ecosystem.

Core System Objectives

Centralized Recovery: Consolidating scattered lost and found reports into a single, high-fidelity platform.
AI Multimodal Matching: Comparing images, semantic text tokens, brand/color attributes, and temporal/geographical proximity.
SecretProof™ Verification: Guarding item handovers with private ownership evidence verification to prevent fraudulent claims.
QR Custody Chain: Providing institutional Lost & Found desks with barcode/QR custody audit trails from intake to closure.

Pilot Implementation: Daffodil International University (DIU)

First campus rollout with real-life student and staff integration

The initial pilot phase is established at DIU Ashulia Campus. Students report lost laptops, wallets, and ID cards, while library desks and campus security desk personnel manage custody records via QR codes. Real metrics are analyzed before wider expansion to railway stations, hospitals, and transit fleets.
